Support

Questions, ideas or a problem? We'd love to hear from you. Every message is read by a real person.

Write to us at dewleafapp@gmail.com or use Settings › Contact us in the app.

Common questions

My reminders don't arrive.

Check Settings › Reminders in DewLeaf, then make sure notifications for DewLeaf are allowed in the iPhone Settings app.

How do I reset my password?

On the sign-in screen tap Forgot password? and we'll email you a link.

How do I delete my account?

In DewLeaf open Settings › Delete account. Your account and backup are deleted straight away; plants on your phone stay.

Can I add more than 5 plants?

The free version looks after up to 5 plants. DewLeaf Plus, with unlimited plants, is coming soon.

A plant I own isn't in the list.

Add it as a custom plant, and tell us its name through Contact us so we can add it.
